🌐 CJ Dropshipping 7–15 days

▼
Products: IDs 277–315 — lifestyle, tech, wellness, fashion. You have an active CJ account. Orders are placed through their app or dashboard.
💳 Make sure your CJ wallet has enough balance BEFORE placing orders. Top up if needed under Wallet → Add Funds.
1
Go to app.cjdropshipping.com and log in.
2
Click My Products in the left sidebar. Find the product the customer ordered (search by name).
3
Click the product → List to Store is optional; instead click Source & Connect or use the Create Order button directly.
4
Go to Orders → Create Order (top right). Search for the product, select the correct variant (size/color), set Qty to 1.
5
Enter the customer's FULL shipping address. Double-check the address — CJ ships to exactly what you enter.
6
Shipping method: select CJPacket Ordinary for US (cheapest) or CJPacket Sensitive if product has battery/liquid. Estimated 7–15 business days.
7
Review order total (charged to your CJ wallet). Click Submit Order and confirm payment.
8
CJ ships within 1–3 business days. Track at CJ → Orders → your order → Tracking Info.
9
Once tracking appears, copy it → Back Office → Fulfillment Hub → Enter Tracking Number above.

✨ Luxury Play — 1on1Wholesale 7–10 business days

▼
Products: Luxury Play tab — premium adult products. Ships from international warehouse. Always communicate 7–10 business days to the customer — noted on all Luxury Play product pages.
Never include the supplier name in any customer-facing communication. All orders are from Dripping Secrets.
1
Log into your account at 1on1wholesale.com.
2
Search for the product by name or SKU from the customer's order. Confirm variant (size/type/color) matches exactly.
3
Add to cart and proceed to checkout. Select Dropship as the order type.
4
Enter the customer's complete shipping address — name, full address, city, state, ZIP. Double-check before submitting.
5
Complete payment at wholesale price using the payment method on file in your account.
6
Save the order confirmation number. Tracking typically arrives within 2–4 business days via email.
7
Once tracking is available, copy it → Back Office → Fulfillment Hub → Enter Tracking Number. The system emails the customer automatically.

Volume 0 — Platform Constitution (The Six Pillars)

Every feature, decision, and build must support these six permanent pillars:

  1. Luxury Shopping — Premium experience, never cheap, never cluttered, never generic.
  2. Education — Customers leave knowing more. Education builds trust, trust builds loyalty.
  3. Experiences — Secrets Parties, Private Shopping, Consultations, Events, VIP Services. Experience = just as important as the product.
  4. Community — Customers eventually feel like members, not buyers.
  5. Technology — Automation feels invisible. Technology simplifies — never complicates.
  6. Dimi — The heartbeat of the platform. Digital concierge, not a chatbot.

The Never-Break List (these systems are never touched without approval):

  • Checkout · Orders · Authentication · Customer Accounts · Products & Product IDs
  • Firebase · Firestore · Netlify Functions · EmailJS · Twilio
  • PayPal · CashApp · Apple Pay · UPS Integration
  • Dimi · Analytics · Accessibility · SEO · URLs · Admin Access
Before every deployment: Create snapshot → Create rollback package → Verify integrations → Then deploy. Never deploy directly without rollback capability.

🎯 Candidate Concierge — Hiring Pipeline

Track every candidate from application to first day. Pipeline stages: Applied → Under Review → Interview → Offer Sent → Onboarding → Hired.

How to use it:

  1. Click + New Candidate and enter their info.
  2. Use the → Next button to advance them through the pipeline.
  3. Click 📝 Offer to pre-fill an offer letter — it opens Document Studio automatically.
  4. Stage counts at the top update live so you can see your pipeline at a glance.

All candidate records save to Firestore under the candidates collection.

🎫 Customer Support — Ticket Tracker

Log and resolve customer issues, refund requests, delivery problems, and product questions — all in one place.

How to use it:

  1. Click + New Ticket → fill in customer info, issue type, and description.
  2. Tickets auto-number (starting at #1001) and track priority.
  3. Click ✓ Resolve to mark a ticket done — counts update instantly.
  4. Use the filter bar to view only Open, Pending, or Resolved tickets.

📡 Communications — Announcements, Log & Campaigns

Three sub-tabs: Announcements (push to SK portal), Message Log (email/SMS/call record), Campaign Records (marketing history).

Announcements:

Message Log:

Manually log any outreach — email, SMS, phone, DM — for your records. Keeps everyone accountable.

Campaign Records:

Track social and marketing campaigns with platform, reach, and status fields.

✍️ E-Signatures — Live Signing with Fonts

Every signable document in the Back Office uses the live e-signature modal. No third-party service — built right in.

Two signing modes:

  • Draw: Sign with your mouse or finger on the canvas.
  • Type: Type your name and pick from 4 signing fonts (Great Vibes, Dancing Script, Allura, Sacramento).

Signatures are injected directly into PDFs and signature records are stored in Firestore with signer name, title, and timestamp.
