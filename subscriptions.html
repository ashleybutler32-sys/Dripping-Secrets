Common Questions

Everything you need to know.

When will my first box ship? +
Your first box ships on the 1st of the following month. Subscribe before the 25th of the month to get that month's box.
How do I pause or skip a month? +
Log into your account, go to the Subscription tab, and click "Manage." You can pause, skip, or cancel with one click — no phone calls, no hassle.
What's in each box? +
Each box is curated to your plan. We never reveal the exact contents in advance — that's part of the fun. Everything is body-safe, brand-new, and quality-verified by our team.
Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan? +
Yes! Upgrade or downgrade anytime from your account. Changes take effect on the next billing cycle.
Is packaging really discreet? +
Completely. All orders ship in plain packaging with no Dripping Secrets branding visible on the outside. Your secret is safe.
What payment methods do you accept? +
We accept CashApp, PayPal, and Apple Pay for subscriptions.
