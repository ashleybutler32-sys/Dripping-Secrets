There Is No Wrong Place to Start

The biggest thing holding most beginners back from exploring intimate wellness is the feeling that they might get it wrong. Wrong product, wrong choice, wrong starting point. Here is the truth: there is no wrong place to start, only places that are more or less right for where you are right now.

This guide is built to give you clarity. Not to overwhelm, not to push you toward anything, but to give you the information you need to make a confident first choice — whatever that looks like for you.

What Makes a Product "Beginner-Friendly"?

Three things: approachable design, body-safe materials, and a satisfying experience that does not require previous knowledge to achieve. Beginner-friendly does not mean basic. It means well-designed for someone who is starting their journey, with quality that is worth your investment.

At Dripping Secrets, we specifically curate for this. Our Beginner-Friendly collection is filtered for products that meet all three criteria — whether you are shopping for a solo product, something for a couple, or a starting point for intimate exploration.

What to Look for in Any Beginner Product

  • Body-safe materials: Medical-grade silicone, ABS plastic, or TPE. Avoid anything unbranded or with a strong plastic smell.
  • Rechargeable: Battery-operated products are fine, but rechargeable is more convenient and more cost-effective over time.
  • Waterproof: Waterproof products are easier to clean and give you more options for use.
  • Multiple intensities: Start low. Being able to start gentle and work up is important for beginners.
  • Clear instructions: Well-designed products come with clear care and use instructions. If a product page cannot tell you how to clean it, that is a signal.
