Privacy Policy

How Dripping Secrets collects, uses, and protects your information.

Effective Date: June 17, 2026  |  Last Updated: June 17, 2026

1. Who We Are

Dripping Secrets is an online adult boutique operated by Ashley Butler, based in the Dallas–Fort Worth, Texas area. Our website is located at drippingsecrets.com. You can reach us at Assistant.Manager@DrippingSecrets.com.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account information: name, email address, and password when you create an account.
  • Order information: shipping address, order contents, and payment method selection.
  • Payment data: we do not store payment card details. Payments are processed through CashApp, PayPal, and Apple Pay — please review their respective privacy policies.
  • Usage data: pages visited, products viewed, and cart activity to improve your experience.
  • Communications: messages you send via our contact form or customer chat.
  • Party booking details: event date, address, headcount, and selected add-on services.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and fulfill your orders and party bookings.
  • To send order confirmations, shipping updates, and tracking numbers.
  • To maintain your account, order history, wishlist, and rewards.
  • To respond to your questions and support requests.
  • To send you promotional offers — only if you opt in.
  • To improve our website, product selection, and customer experience.

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share it only with:

  • Fulfillment partners (dropship suppliers) — limited to the shipping information needed to deliver your order. Supplier names are never disclosed to customers.
  • Payment processors (CashApp, PayPal, Apple Pay) — as required to complete transactions.
  • Email service providers — to send transactional and notification emails on our behalf.
  • Legal obligations — if required by law, court order, or to protect our rights.

5. Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies and local storage to keep you signed in, remember your cart, and understand how visitors use our site. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some features may not work as expected.

6. Data Retention

We retain your account and order data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by emailing us.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data, or to opt out of marketing communications. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at Assistant.Manager@DrippingSecrets.com.

8. Children's Privacy

Dripping Secrets is an adult boutique. Our website is intended for individuals 18 years of age and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

9. Third-Party Links

Our site may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites.

10. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including HTTPS encryption and Firebase Authentication to protect your data. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of our site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
