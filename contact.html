Yes! We offer same-day personal delivery in the DFW Metro area for orders over $50. Local delivery is $15. You'll select your delivery slot at checkout.
Is my packaging discreet?▾
Absolutely. All orders ship in plain, unmarked packaging. Your privacy is our priority — no brand names or product descriptions on the outside of the box.
How do I pay?▾
